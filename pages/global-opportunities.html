Civitas Global Opportunities Program

About the Program

Bringing opportunities closer to students.

What is it?

This program connects students with opportunities beyond their immediate university environment, including scholarships, exchanges, fellowships, competitions, conferences, internships and other cross-campus or international opportunities.

Opportunities

Greater exposure to opportunities, networks and experiences.

Who is it for?

Students interested in expanding their academic, professional and cultural experiences.
