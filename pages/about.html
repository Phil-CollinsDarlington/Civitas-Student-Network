About CSN

Who We Are

Civitas Student Network Ltd (CSN) is a student-focused network connecting young people to opportunities, knowledge, mentorship, professional networks and meaningful experiences.

Our story

Born in Uganda. Connected to the world.

CSN exists to create a supportive community where students can develop their potential and prepare for life beyond the classroom.

We are building a connected ecosystem where students, universities, professionals, organisations and businesses can interact, share knowledge and create opportunities together.

Our purpose

Why We Exist

We help students discover relevant opportunities, connect with the right people, build the skills to pursue them and gain meaningful experience.

Vision

Vision

A future where students are connected to a world of opportunity, equipped to realise their potential and empowered to create meaningful change.

Mission

Mission

Civitas Student Network connects students across institutions with mentors, professionals, organisations, knowledge and opportunities that help them develop skills, gain experience, build relationships and prepare for life beyond the classroom.

Our Values

Core Values

Excellence

Holding ourselves and our work to a high standard.

Integrity

Acting honestly, consistently and transparently.

Collaboration

Creating opportunities through shared knowledge and collective action.

Inclusion

Making sure the network is open and accessible to every student.

Innovation

Encouraging new ideas and practical solutions to real-world challenges.

Leadership

Developing confident, responsible and ethical leaders.

Empowerment

Equipping students to take ownership of their futures.

Global Citizenship

Thinking and connecting beyond borders to create meaningful change.

Our journey

Uganda to Global

Today: A Ugandan student network

CSN starts by connecting students across Uganda with opportunities, mentors and each other.

Next: Regional growth

Expanding the network to more campuses and communities across the region.

Future: A global student network

One network, endless possibilities — connecting students worldwide and empowering them to create meaningful change.

Our ecosystem

More than a student community

CSN creates a structured pathway between students and the wider world of opportunity.

Who we serve

Our primary community is students and young people. The network also brings together universities and educational institutions, professionals and mentors, companies and employers, NGOs, entrepreneurs, innovators and international and community partners.

Who we work with

We welcome partnerships that create meaningful opportunities for students and contribute to education, employability, leadership, innovation and community development.

Our Future

CSN is built to grow — from a Ugandan student initiative into a global network that any student, anywhere, can be part of.
