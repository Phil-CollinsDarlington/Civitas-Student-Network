Civitas Entrepreneurship and Innovation Program

Supports students building ventures, products and solutions to real problems.

About the Program

Turning ideas into possibilities

What is it?

This program supports students who want to explore entrepreneurship, develop ideas and create practical solutions to real-world problems.

Who is it for?

Aspiring entrepreneurs, innovators, student founders and solution-builders.

What do students gain?

Entrepreneurial thinking, confidence and practical skills to move ideas toward action.

What We Cover

From idea to action

Idea Development

Helping students identify and refine ideas that address real-world problems.

Innovation Challenges

Opportunities for students to apply entrepreneurial thinking to real-world problems and challenges.

Entrepreneurial Networks

Connecting students with mentors, peers and industry experts to support their entrepreneurial journey.

Entrepreneurship workshops

Workshops and talks that help students develop entrepreneurial skills, confidence and practical know-how.

Business concept development

Helping students turn ideas into practical business concepts and solutions.

Pitching Opportunities

Opportunities for students to pitch ideas, receive feedback and connect with potential partners or investors.

Founder conversations

Conversations with student founders, entrepreneurs and innovators to inspire and guide students.

Entrepreneurship resources

Access to resources, tools and guides to support students in their entrepreneurial journey.
